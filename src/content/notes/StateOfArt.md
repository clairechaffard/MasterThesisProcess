A map of the theories, projects, methods that already exists around your topic so you can see where you stand and where a gap remains, reveal your point of view. 

Examples of materials : 
- theory : book articles debates (key definition, concepts)
- practice : projects, prototypes places collectives initiatives products 
- method : how things were done researched (historical, field research)
  
Bring your perspective, you can take another angle, look into a different context or audience. 

## choosing the topic 
How to wrtie a thesis - Umberto Eco 
pick a topic that matches your interests, your language skills and the sources 

make it narrow, th emore you limit the filed the better and safer you will work 
"the novel since WWII" is too broad a specific author, text, or problem is better.

## How to find relevant articles ? 
When gathering your references, analyse them, why is it interesting ? What stands out ? 

When starting might use imprecise terms, words that differ from the specific terminology chosen by researchers or experts to describe that phenomenon

tips : 
- look into bibliographies of books and articles you are interested in : check for sources 
- look inoto conderences line up, festivals prgm, prizes results to discover new artists, designers, studios or researchers
- keep on eye on key museums you like 
  
- you must dstinguish between primary sources and secondary sources. 
- must understand the status of the author : are they recognized expert in the discipline or subject matter ? are they a designer or a researcher 
- where was the text published ? is it personal or commercial blog
