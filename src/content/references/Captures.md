note: "Captures is an audiovisual performance that investigates the memory of places through 3D scans and point clouds, at the frontier between reality and generative environments. Architectural fragments and ephemeral details are reassembled into shifting visual worlds, while sound interacts with these forms to create a dialogue between space and perception. Rather than documenting reality, Captures reimagines it, creating a space where environments and field recordings overlap, dissolve, and reappear. It suggests a living archive where architectures are continuously recomposed.The project is made to be adapted to different setups, live & exported. 2D live A/V version made for the Fog Festival"
